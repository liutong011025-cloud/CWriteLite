## Flow

Original login (no role selector) → original My Farm → original Writing Map (Story pin only) → choose saved characters / create → Story Canvas → Start writing → five writing sections → review and save to map. The Freytag mountain remains available as an optional planning view. Characters are saved independently of each story. Completed stories can inspire the original map image update and farm growth, using the original providers. Opening the app displays login even when a session cookie exists.

Character: choose an original species emoji (or enter a custom species), draw, then fill name, optional age, appearance and traits. The species is stored on the character card and passed to image generation. Background/role, strength and challenge remain optional. AI Tips has no topic selector: focus follows the input field; chips explicitly add words to that field. The tall tips panel replaces the previous menu. A story goal belongs to the canvas. Existing stories keep character snapshots.

Drawing and typed appearance information feed the same Fal image-edit / image generation model used in CWrite. Students explicitly choose a portrait before saving. AI vocabulary chips add words to editable fields only when the student taps them.

Canvas: draggable character, object, goal and note cards; arrows have editable relationship labels. Portraits use contain sizing so the whole character remains visible. The most recently added setting with an image supplies the background under an ivory veil. Once illustrated, that setting no longer renders as a separate card; its scene information remains in the saved writing plan. AI connections target visible cards only. Students explicitly use or skip each proposal. The repeated Cagent chat panel is removed from both canvas and writing; planning ideas and the upper writing mascot remain. The separate writing feedback button is removed.

Character Idea Pack sits above the left edge of the deck. Its gold/olive foil wrapper uses the original `logosmall.webp`, serrated seals and a wavy tear line. Gentle occasional wiggle and shine respect reduced-motion settings. Clicking opens characters immediately in an in-page modal. Login and writing headers share the new white/gold logo with a small tilted yellow cursive lite. Farm branding is shifted to 66% of viewport width; Hello there is capped at 20px. Primary buttons use olive, secondary buttons warm ivory.

Connections: each card has four corner anchors. Drag a corner to a corner on another card; a visible line follows the pointer, and releasing over a different card opens the relationship label input. Selected source and target corners are saved with the edge and stay attached when cards move or resize. Connect mode also allows dragging anywhere on a card. Keyboard users can select two anchors with Enter or Space. AI suggestions simultaneously appear as pale glowing dashed lines on the canvas; only an explicit click or Enter adopts a proposal. Labels wrap and seek space away from other labels and cards, within board bounds. Extremely crowded boards may still require the student to spread cards out.

Writing Cagent uses the saved canvas, character traits, goals, relationships, previous sections and current draft. Its short question updates after a 2.4-second typing pause, with an immediate plan-based local fallback. It never writes the paragraph for the student. Next Section flushes the draft, then checks intelligibility, section purpose and a meaningful connection to the plan. Simple sentences, paraphrase, pronouns, spelling and grammar errors are allowed. Repeated words, copying the previous section, unrelated content and insufficient development need revision. All five sections require current server approvals before completing a story. Approval signatures change when the draft or meaningful plan changes; moving a card alone does not invalidate them. Students cannot create approval events or advance via a direct save request. AI failure preserves the draft and offers retry, rather than passing it automatically.

Writing: the student clicks Get Suggestions. The current paragraph near the cursor, current section, story text, character snapshots and canvas are passed to the server. AI infers the current focus. Output is words / short phrases and sentence frames with blanks. Students do not choose a topic category. Vocabulary and Sentence Structures tabs only switch output formats. Try Again changes suggestions and never rewrites the draft. Tapping a suggestion is an explicit insertion, with an undo action.

## Academic rationale — evidence and design inference

1. Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976). The role of tutoring in problem solving. *Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17*(2), 89–100. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x
   The foundational tutoring paper provides the scaffolding concept. Its original task was not AI writing. Our brief prompts and decomposition into manageable steps are design applications, not a finding that these particular interfaces improve learning.

2. van de Pol, J., Volman, M., & Beishuizen, J. (2010). Scaffolding in teacher–student interaction: A decade of research. *Educational Psychology Review, 22*, 271–296. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9127-6
   The review identifies contingency, fading and transfer of responsibility. Context-sensitive suggestions operationalize contingency; optional requests and dismissal support learner control. This version does **not** automatically implement or establish fading. A longitudinal reduction of support and independent transfer tasks would be needed to investigate it.

3. Harris, K. R., Graham, S., & Mason, L. H. (2006). Improving the writing, knowledge, and motivation of struggling young writers: Effects of self-regulated strategy development with and without peer support. *American Educational Research Journal, 43*(2), 295–340. https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312043002295
   The study concerns strategy instruction for young writers, including story writing. Explicit planning and revision prompts draw on this direction. CWrite Lite is not an implementation of the complete SRSD intervention; a canvas or mountain alone does not reproduce it.

Names/ages/traits are practical character representation choices. No cited study establishes these exact three fields as a validated elementary-school character schema. The mountain is a narrative organizing convention selected by the user, not claimed to be the only or best structure for every K–12 learner. Visual card collection and reusable characters are testable design hypotheses.

4. Council of Europe, [CEFR descriptors: General linguistic range, A1](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors-search). A1 descriptions allow very basic expressions and one-clause structures with missing elements. This informs accepting understandable imperfect EFL language. The section gate is a formative product design, **not** a CEFR assessment or a validated K–12 proficiency measure. Its 12-word minimum is a pragmatic anti-empty-draft threshold, not a research-derived norm; it should be calibrated with learners of different ages and proficiency levels. AI judgments remain fallible.

## Research-ready records

`ResearchEvent` stores request kind, writing stage, cursor-near context, suggestions shown, accepted/skipped/shuffled/dismissed actions, node/connection actions and feedback/chat requests. `Revision` stores timestamped section versions. AI generation events store request identifiers and image references. Results are linked to a user and optional story. Teacher-only export includes events and revisions. Usage clicks indicate exposure and choices; they do not by themselves prove learning, motivation or successful strategy internalization.

Possible initial study: hold UI, writing prompts, time and model constant; randomize keyword-only versus keywords-plus-frames scaffolds, with teacher lessons documented. Pretest, posttest and a delayed **no-AI** new-story transfer task should be scored blind using a preregistered rubric (narrative coherence, goal–action–consequence links, language accuracy, lexical diversity), with inter-rater agreement. Analyze draft revisions and suggestions uptake as process evidence. Research conditions and automatic fading are future configurable interventions, not already deployed experimental features. Obtain the institution's research approval and participant/guardian arrangements before recruitment.
